# Where Dispersed 3D is, and where it is headed

Play the live game free at **[momatio.com/dispersed3d](https://momatio.com/dispersed3d/)**. The newest things show up first in **[the preview](https://momatio.com/dispersed3d/try)**. This page is the whole list, in plain words. Every release is also in the [changelog](https://github.com/davelalande/dispersed3d-progress/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Live

Everything below is in the game you can play today, newest release first.

### November 18, 2026, 2:09 PM: A second desert, Hurricane Cliffs, Utah

- **Hurricane Cliffs, UT.** A new free-camping desert on BLM land near St. George: eight camps, dry washes and red-rock ground. Pick it in the world list.
- **Open now, until November 30.** The game plays this place March through May and October through November, the months when the nights normally stay above freezing and the days stay under 90. The window is the game's own, set from the weather service's normal nights; the land office states no closure here. Outside it the list reads "Back Mar 1".
- **Nobody camps in a wash.** If a recorded camp sits in a dry wash with no road to pull in beside, your pad is moved out onto higher ground nearby (two camps here move: about 118 feet and 20 feet). Flash floods are real, so the game does what you would do.
- More in the [changelog](https://github.com/davelalande/dispersed3d-progress/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### November 18, 2026, 12:02 PM: The mouse steers, and the top of the screen folds away

- **The mouse steers.** On a computer, hold W and move the mouse: the truck turns to follow the camera, then straightens by itself. A and D still steer and always win. Hold the right mouse button, or Z, X or Q, to look around without steering. Parked, the mouse looks around as before. "Steer with the mouse" in Help turns it off.
- **The truck eases off for a tight bend.** Hold the wheel hard over and it slows for the corner, then picks its speed back up when you straighten out.
- **Hide the top of the screen.** One tap on Hide folds the camp card, the gauges and the compass into one thin line: the clock, the light left, your fuel and water, and how far to camp. It stays folded until you tap Show.
- More in the [changelog](https://github.com/davelalande/dispersed3d-progress/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### November 18, 2026, 8:27 AM: The view from above, Play gets you rolling, and walking the way you expect

- **The view from above.** On a computer the game now starts in Chase cam, above and behind the truck, so you see the rig and the road ahead. Cockpit view and Hood cam are a press of F away, and switching eases in over a moment instead of jumping. The view you pick is the one you get next time.
- **Your head turns back to the road by itself** when you let go of the look.
- **W, A, S, D to drive, Shift for more gas.** The arrow keys no longer drive, and the help says so in plain words. The mouse no longer steers unless you switch it on; in the cab it turns your head.
- More in the [changelog](https://github.com/davelalande/dispersed3d-progress/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### November 12, 2026, 8:17 AM: Pack the truck before you leave

- **Pack the truck.** Before the four nights start, one screen asks what goes in the truck: the roof shower tank, filled; the hand-wash jug and soap; the water filter; the camp stove and a full canister; a headlamp; the portable battery and panels; and traction boards. Firewood shows up only where a camp's own record says wood is scarce. Tick what you'd bring, or tap "Take it all." Loading takes no game time, and the gas, the drinking water, the food and the tent are always aboard.
- **Whatever stays home turns up once, kindly,** the first time you'd reach for it. The trip card then says whether you had everything you reached for. Nothing is marked wrong.
- **AI players pack too.** The game's state now says what's aboard, and one new page tool loads the truck. Older challenges and checked runs play exactly as before.

### November 11, 2026, 7:44 AM: The short loop holds still, and Beat the bot counts your days

- **The short walking loop is the same on every device.** The loop the game lays out from camp used to depend on how much fine detail had loaded on your screen when it was planned. A phone and a computer could show different loops from the same camp, and the summit named at the turnaround could change between visits. The loop is now chosen from the same ground map on every device and every visit, and it still never climbs more than 30 percent on the ground you see. Nothing about the walk's length rules or the "this path is the game's, not a mapped trail" note changed.
- **Beat the bot counts your days in a row ([/beat/](https://momatio.com/dispersed3d/beat/)).** Finish all seven decisions and the end says "Day one", then "2 days in a row", "3 days in a row" and so on, and your share line carries it from day two. It counts days you played, never how often you agreed with the AI.
- **A saved trip can only be added to from the browser that started it.** A new trip now gets a private key that stays in your browser. The trip code you share still opens the replay for anyone, but only your browser can keep saving to the trip. Trips started before this update work as they did.

### November 9, 2026, 9:25 PM: Three daily games, and "Ask your AI" on a finished trip

- **Beat the bot (new page, [/beat/](https://momatio.com/dispersed3d/beat/)).** Seven real decisions a day, taken from checked AI runs. You see the place, the clock, the gas, water and light line, and the moves the game offered. You pick, then see what the AI picked and its reason. Your pick and the AI's are shown the same size, and neither is marked "right"; a "Watch this moment" link replays that spot in the game.
- **Name that forest (new page, [/skyline/](https://momatio.com/dispersed3d/skyline/)).** A daily quiz: a drawing of a skyline as seen from camp, and you name the place (four choices: three Montana national forests and Quartzsite, Arizona, which is BLM land). The skylines are drawn with the game's own calculation from the same ground data, so the answer is the one the game itself would show.
- **When does the sun leave this camp? (new page, [/puzzle/](https://momatio.com/dispersed3d/puzzle/)).** A 30-second daily puzzle. You guess the minute the ridge takes the sun away from a real camp, and see how close you were. The answers come from the game's own ridge calculation, not a second guess. Calculations only count the ridge on our map, so a real camp can differ by a few minutes.
- More in the [changelog](https://github.com/davelalande/dispersed3d-progress/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### November 3, 2026, 7:24 AM: In the rain you get inside

- When the Weather Service's hourly forecast says it is raining at your camp, the camp card says so, with the chance and about when it stops. Cooking, dishes and a fire outside still work, but you are standing in it. The card offers the dry ways: **Wait out the rain in the cab** (or inside the van) until the forecast's first dry hour, up to 2 hours; **Get in the tent for the rain** once the tent is up (take what you need and go potty first); **Eat something that needs no stove**; and, once you're inside with the satellite dish up and sky over it, **Watch a show on Starlink**, which draws from the batteries like the dish does
- The trip card gains a row on a trip that saw rain at camp: **Got in out of the rain**. It is checked when nothing was cooked, washed up or lit outside while it rained. The van's galley is inside, so it cooks in any weather. Rain that falls only while you sleep doesn't count
- Camp parking spots now keep off creeks. The aerial photo misses narrow creeks, so the game also reads the USGS creek lines: a spot within 40 feet of a creek is refused when other ground will do, and spots 200 feet away, as Leave No Trace says, are preferred. Morrell Creek's spot in the Lolo moved off the creek bed

## Ideas we're weighing

Nobody is building these yet. Each waits on a decision. If one matters to you, [tell us](https://github.com/davelalande/dispersed3d-progress/issues/new?template=idea.yml).

- **Smaller, faster photos on phones.** Compressed ground photos, so the forest loads quicker and uses less memory. On hold for now.
- **Wildlife: a deer or an elk at dusk.** In the headlights, or down at the creek. We hold no record of which animals live where, so this one needs real range data first, or a plain line saying the animal is the game's.
- **Read this card aloud, in the phone's own voice.** The camp, night, morning and trouble cards get one Read button. The phone speaks the words already on the card; a second tap stops it. It is the browser's voice, not Dave's, and the button says so. If the phone cannot speak, the button is not there.
